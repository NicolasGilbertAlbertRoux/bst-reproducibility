# Verification record

## Audit of the calibrated benchmark

The standard command runs twelve numerical sections, four supplementary checks, the calibrated observatory, the projection rebuild, the atlas and the Lean proof checks. The 576 assemblies were re-evaluated against all 67 contributing clauses. At a common local strontium contrast of 1.05, the same 192 assemblies also pass the specified strontium spacing tests on both isotopes. The [machine-readable results](audit.json) contain all five contrasts, the classification of the original clauses, the scope of the calibration, and the results of the additional rendering and trajectory checks. The separate long command regenerates the upstream field and transport trajectories.
